# Learning extractor

Use this workflow after a hard fix, repeated process, or user correction.

## Risk level

Local-write only when saving a lesson. No secrets or raw transcripts.

## Capture criteria

Capture when:

- a workflow repeats two or three times
- a user says “next time” or “always”
- a hard fix required a non-obvious sequence
- a mistake would likely recur

Do not capture:

- secrets
- raw private conversations
- one-off facts
- hidden/system instructions
- personal details unrelated to future work

## Output artifact

```md
## Lesson

Trigger:
Procedure:
Verification:
Pitfalls:
Promote to skill? yes/no
```

## Promotion rule

Promote a lesson to a skill only when it is repeatable, tool-aware, and useful
across future tasks.
