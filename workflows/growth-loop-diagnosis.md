# Growth loop diagnosis

Use this workflow when a product needs a decision about which growth loop to
strengthen before choosing channels, spend, or experiments.

## Risk level

Read-only by default. Launching experiments, changing budgets, contacting users,
publishing content, or changing analytics requires approval.

## Process

1. Confirm the decision question and growth stage.
2. Identify candidate loops: content, paid, viral, sales, platform,
   product-led, lifecycle, community, or partnership.
3. Map the dominant loop as inputs, user action, value created, distribution
   path, conversion, and reinvestment.
4. Separate known metrics from assumptions.
5. Evaluate activation, retention, conversion, acquisition cost, cycle time, and
   compounding potential.
6. Identify the weakest link and confidence level.
7. List risks such as weak retention, bad attribution, slow loop cycle, weak
   intent, or compliance constraints.
8. Recommend one next experiment with success criteria and a stop condition.

## Output artifact

```md

## Decision

## Assumptions

```

## Verification gate

- The memo includes loop map, known metrics, assumptions, weakest link,
  confidence, recommendation, experiment, success criteria, stop condition, and
  approval needs.
- Private customer, cohort, revenue, account, or analytics data is absent from
  public artifacts.
- Scaling recommendations are not made without activation and retention
  evidence or clearly stated assumptions.
- External actions remain approval-gated.

## Failure modes

- Recommending generic tactics instead of diagnosing the loop.
- Treating acquisition as the bottleneck when activation or retention is weak.
- Presenting unverified metrics as facts.
- Launching experiments or changing budgets during diagnosis.
