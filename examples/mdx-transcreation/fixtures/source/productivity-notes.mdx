Most teams do not need another dashboard. They need a calmer way to notice what
changed.

Weekly notes are useful because they create a small rhythm: what shipped, what
blocked us, what surprised us, and what needs a decision.

```ts
type WeeklyNote = {
  shipped: string[];
  blocked: string[];
  decisions: string[];
};
```

<img
  src="/images/blog/weekly-notes.jpg"

## Make the important work visible

The best weekly note is not a status report. It is a map of attention.

If a project keeps appearing in the blocked section, it probably needs a
decision. If a small win keeps repeating, it may be a process worth keeping.
